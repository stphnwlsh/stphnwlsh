## Monthly tiers

### $3 a month: Kitchen hand

> A small monthly thank you that helps keep the releases coming. You get my thanks, the Sponsor badge on your profile, and a warm fuzzy feeling at no extra charge.

Welcome message:

> Thank you for backing Lean Mean Clean Apps! I send a short update when each release goes out on the 1st. Reply any time with something you'd like to see in the templates.

### $10 a month: Line cook

> Everything in Kitchen hand, plus your name in the Sponsors section of the docs site and the template READMEs. You'll also get a first look at what's planned for the next release.

Welcome message:

> Thank you, welcome to the line! I'll add your name to the Sponsors list in the next release. If you'd rather stay anonymous, just reply and say so.

### $25 a month: The sous-chef

> Everything in Line cook, plus a vote on what goes into the next release. Tell me which template, feature or fix matters to you and it goes to the top of the list I look at first. (Named after Jacques, the persona our agents work as.)

Welcome message:

> Merci, sous-chef! Reply with the one thing you'd most like in the templates and I'll put it at the top of the list. I can't promise every idea ships, but I'll always tell you why.

### $100 a month: The kitchen brigade

> For companies building on Lean Mean Clean Apps. Your logo goes on the docs site and the template READMEs, and you get a 30-minute call each quarter about how you're using the templates. It's not a support contract, but it is a direct line to the person who wrote them.

Welcome message:

> Thank you for backing Lean Mean Clean Apps! Reply with your logo (SVG please) and the link you'd like it to point to, and we'll find a time for your first call.
