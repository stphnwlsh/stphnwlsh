## Intro

> Hello! I'm Steve, a software engineering leader in Neenah, Wisconsin, by way of Australia.
>
> For fun, I look after **Lean Mean Clean Apps**, a small family of `dotnet new` templates with a low-ceremony take on Clean Architecture: fewer layers than an onion, more than a sandwich. There are five so far: Minimal API, MCP server, GraphQL (Hot Chocolate), Web API and Lambda Function. They share the same Application and Infrastructure layers, so learn one and you know them all.
>
> Every template is ready for the real world: .NET 10, a 95% coverage gate, CodeQL and Trivy scans, optional token auth, Scalar docs, and a set of agents and skills that teach Claude Code, Copilot or Cursor how we build things, so your AI helpers write code that looks like your team wrote it.
>
> It's all free and MIT licensed, and it'll stay that way. A release goes out on the 1st of every month and a maintenance release on the 15th, which takes evenings, weekends and a frankly irresponsible amount of coffee.
>
> If the templates have saved you a day of setup, sponsoring is the nicest way to say so. It keeps the releases coming and tells me people are actually using them, which is half the fun.
>
> Docs: https://leanmeancleanapps.github.io

## Monthly tiers

### $3 a month: The flat white

> Shouts me a coffee each month, which is roughly what one release runs on. You get my thanks, the Sponsor badge on your profile, and a warm fuzzy feeling at no extra charge.

Welcome message:

> Thank you! That's this month's coffee sorted. I send a short update when each release goes out on the 1st. Reply any time with something you'd like to see in the templates.

### $10 a month: The regular

> Everything in The flat white, plus your name in the Sponsors section of the docs site and the template READMEs. You'll also get a first look at what's planned for the next release.

Welcome message:

> Thank you, you're officially a regular! I'll add your name to the Sponsors list in the next release. If you'd rather stay anonymous, just reply and say so.

### $25 a month: The sous-chef

> Everything in The regular, plus a vote on what goes into the next release. Tell me which template, feature or fix matters to you and it goes to the top of the list I look at first. (Named after Jacques, the persona our agents work as.)

Welcome message:

> Merci, sous-chef! Reply with the one thing you'd most like in the templates and I'll put it at the top of the list. I can't promise every idea ships, but I'll always tell you why.

### $100 a month: The kitchen brigade

> For companies building on Lean Mean Clean Apps. Your logo goes on the docs site and the template READMEs, and you get a 30-minute call each quarter about how you're using the templates. It's not a support contract, but it is a direct line to the person who wrote them.

Welcome message:

> Thank you for backing Lean Mean Clean Apps! Reply with your logo (SVG please) and the link you'd like it to point to, and we'll find a time for your first call.

## When the first sponsor arrives

The $10 tier and up promise a Sponsors section on the docs site and in the template READMEs. Add it with the first sponsor, not before, because an empty list looks lonely. Keep the copy here in step with what the sections actually show.
